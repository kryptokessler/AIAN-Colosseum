text = """DHL Aims to Simplify U.S. Customs with New Consolidated Clearance Service

BONN, GERMANY – DHL Global Forwarding has launched a new service to streamline the customs process for goods entering the United States. The division of the global logistics company DHL Group announced the "DHL consolidated clearance service" in a recent press release.

The new offering is designed to assist U.S. retailers and importers facing challenges from shifting global trade patterns and increasingly complex tariff regulations. By consolidating multiple individual shipments under a single customs entry, the service aims to create a more cost-efficient and simplified clearance process.

This initiative allows businesses to manage their import operations more effectively, addressing the logistical and financial hurdles associated with processing numerous separate shipments at U.S. ports of entry."""
